# ADR-016 — The corpus freeze and the living-document boundary
**2026-07-10 · Accepted** — governance decision required by the [2026-07-10 architecture review](../reviews/2026-07-10-architecture-review.md) §2; first ADR recorded in the `decisions\` directory.

**Context.** On 2026-07-10 the operator declared the legacy corpus in `D:\Fable OS` canonical and frozen: never rewritten, regenerated, or merged; future improvement happens through new ADRs and implementation. Taken literally, this collides with three of the corpus's own contracts: DECISIONS.md declares itself append-only ("Next entry: ADR-009. Append below"); Law 8 *requires* COMPONENTS.md updates in the same session as any change; ROADMAP.md instructs both operator and AI to tick its checkboxes. Left ambiguous, the first working session must violate either the freeze or Law 8 — and whichever violation happens first becomes precedent.

**Decision.** The freeze has a precise boundary:

1. **Frozen (historical artifacts — never edited, byte-stable):** FABLE.md, FABLE-MENTAL-MODELS.md, all fifteen `*_ENGINE.md` documents, ARCHITECTURE.md, ARCHITECTURE-V2.md, CONSTITUTION.md, README.md, DECISIONS.md, existing files under `skills\` and `reviews\`, and `install\phase-0.ps1`. Constitutional amendment happens as the constitution itself specifies — by ADR — with the law's text unedited; superseded content is superseded by reference, never by deletion.
2. **Living (operational registries, edited only in their designed modes):** COMPONENTS.md (row updates, per Law 8), ROADMAP.md (checkbox ticks and appended amendment pointers only), MAINTENANCE.md (until superseded by OPERATIONS.md), `.gitignore`, and everything under `config\`, `memory\`, `journal\`, `vault\`, `projects\`, `outbox\`.
3. **The ADR ledger continues as a directory.** New ADRs are one file each in `decisions\` (`ADR-###.md`), numbered continuously from DECISIONS.md's last entry (ADR-008). DECISIONS.md itself stays byte-frozen; `decisions\README.md` is the ledger's index and discovery point. ADRs remain append-only: never edited after acceptance, only superseded by a later ADR.
4. **New artifacts beside frozen ones are permitted:** new files in `install\`, `tests\`, `skills\` (per ADR-023), `reviews\`, and new top-level documents (SECURITY.md, PRIVACY.md, GLOSSARY.md, OPERATIONS.md, …) do not violate the freeze. Superseding an obsolete frozen artifact (e.g. `install\phase-0b.ps1` over `phase-0.ps1`) is done by a new file plus an ADR or registry note — the old file stays as history.

**Consequences.** Law 8 and the freeze are both satisfiable, permanently. Readers get one rule: *methodology is history; registries are state; decisions accrete in `decisions\`.* Cost: README.md's directory map will drift from reality as new directories appear — accepted; current navigation lives in `decisions\README.md` and, later, OPERATIONS.md. A frozen document that is *wrong* (e.g. phase-0.ps1's known defects) stays visibly wrong, superseded beside it rather than fixed in place — that is the price and the point of a historical record.

**Alternatives considered.** (1) *Append new ADRs directly to DECISIONS.md* — rejected: the operator's freeze language ("not by editing the legacy corpus") is best honored by byte-stability, and one-file-per-ADR is easier for future agents to diff, cite, and traverse (it also feeds the ADR-015 decisions graph naturally). (2) *Parallel living registries (e.g. `state\COMPONENTS.md`) with legacy registries frozen* — rejected: a permanent two-places-to-look tax on every future agent and a standing risk of the frozen copy being read as current. (3) *No formal boundary, case-by-case judgment* — rejected: that is a wish, and this system has documented what wishes cost.

**Future review criteria.** Reopen if: (a) a frozen document's staleness causes an operational error despite a superseding artifact existing — the discovery mechanism (this ADR's item 3/4) failed and needs strengthening; (b) the count of superseded-in-place artifacts grows past roughly a dozen — consider an `attic\` convention (by new ADR) rather than deletion; (c) any future operator directive redefines the freeze — this ADR is superseded, not edited.
